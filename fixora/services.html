How It Works

From Booking to Fixed in Four Simple Steps

A clear repair journey from the moment you request service until your appliance is working again.

1

Tell Us What's Wrong

Pick your appliance, describe the fault in your own words and choose a visit slot. Our system pre-screens symptoms so your technician arrives with the right parts already on the van.

  • Book online in under 60 seconds, or call us
  • Same-day slots for bookings before 2 PM
  • Instant SMS confirmation with your repair ID
2

Meet Your Technician

You'll receive your technician's name, photo, certification badges and a live ETA. When they're en route, watch them approach on the map from your dashboard.

  • Background-checked, factory-certified pros only
  • Live ETA updates from dispatch to doorstep
  • Shoe covers and drop cloths as standard
3

Approve Your Itemised Quote

The technician performs a full diagnosis, then sends an itemised digital quote to your phone: parts, labour and tax listed separately. Nothing proceeds until you tap Approve.

  • $39 inspection fee waived with any repair
  • Genuine OEM parts with prices shown upfront
  • Honest replace-vs-repair advice if it applies
4

Pay After It's Fixed

We test the appliance with you, hand over your digital invoice and register your 90-day warranty automatically. Payment happens only once you're satisfied.

  • Cards, UPI, bank transfer or cash
  • Digital invoice stored in your dashboard forever
  • 90-day warranty on parts and workmanship
