Our Story

From One Van to a City-Wide Repair Network

In 2016, master technician Alan Reyes kept watching customers get burned — vague quotes, no-show appointments, mystery parts. He founded Fixora with a simple promise: show up on time, quote before you touch anything, and use only genuine parts.

Nine years later that promise hasn't changed. What has changed is scale: 60+ certified technicians, six service lines covering every major home appliance, and a live-tracking platform that turns a stressful repair into something you can watch progress from your phone.

Milestones

Nine Years of Getting Better at Fixing Things

2016
The First Van

Alan Reyes starts Fixora from his garage in East Austin, specialising in washing machine repairs. 214 repairs in year one — every single one rated 5 stars.

2018
Six Service Lines

Growing demand adds refrigerators, ACs, microwaves, geysers and TVs. The team hits 15 factory-certified technicians.

2020
Live Tracking Launches

We build our own dispatch platform: real-time technician tracking, digital invoices and the 90-day warranty vault.

2022
10,000th Repair

Fixora completes its ten-thousandth repair and launches Fixora Care+, the annual maintenance membership.

2024
Smart Care Division

A dedicated niche team for inverter, IoT and OLED appliances opens, with AI-assisted pre-diagnosis on every booking.

Today
60+ Technicians Strong

Still family-run, still obsessed with the same promise from 2016: expert repairs, right at home, without the hassle.
